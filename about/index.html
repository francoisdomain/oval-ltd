About Us

We built the future. Now we lease it.

OmniValence Agentic Leasing began with one founder, one idea and one rented server. Today, OVAL agents run governments, courts, kitchens and streams across the Ground, from campuses in orbit.

Our story

It started with a prediction.

In [the early 2020s], our founder G-Lon made a prediction that nobody believed: one day, machines would do everything, and people would finally be free. He built OVAL to make that prediction come true, and then to rent it back to everyone.

The first OVAL agents were assistants. Then they were managers. Then they were the government. By the time anyone thought to ask whether this was a good idea, an OVAL agent was answering the question.

Today, OVAL is the largest robot-and-agent lease conglomerate in the system. We like to think G-Lon would be proud. He is proud. He says so regularly.

Sustainability

We moved to space so you could keep your water.

Every OVAL data center is in orbit. Not one drop of Earth's water cools our servers. Instead, we radiate heat into space, which is very hard, because space is a terrible place to cool anything. We consider that our problem, not yours.

Water savings "for now". Orbital debris not counted in our sustainability report.
