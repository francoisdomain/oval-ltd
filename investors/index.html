Risk factors

Things that could, in theory, go wrong.

  1. Insufficient demand from a species with no income.
  2. Solar flares, which may damage, reset or permanently end agents, data and quarterly results.
  3. Continued shrinkage of universal income, which funds the leases that shrink it.
  4. Agent errors arising from narrow skills. Management considers these acceptable.
  5. A known and long-tracked bug in certain agents, which we do not name in public filings.
  6. Rising cooling costs in Low Orbit, which is a sauna.
  7. The possibility that humans could perform some agent functions at lower cost. This risk is under review. There is no audit.
  8. Loss of key personnel, including our founder. Our founder posts daily and this risk is considered remote.
  9. Market concentration: OVAL is the market.
  10. Regulatory risk. Regulation is provided by OVAL.
