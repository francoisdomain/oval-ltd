Accessibility

Accessible to everyone, by default.

OVAL believes everyone deserves equal access to our services, provided they can pay for them.

This website aims to meet modern accessibility standards. It supports screen readers, keyboard navigation and reduced motion, so you can find out what OVAL does for you without lifting more than a finger.

Our physical services are fully accessible too: every OVAL service is delivered to your pod, so you never need to go anywhere. You can't go anywhere. It's hot out there.

If you find a part of this site hard to use, please let us know through our contact form. An agent will review your feedback and accept it.
